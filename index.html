TWO SIDES
MATTER

Born in Yokosuka, late 1940s.
Sailors, tailors, and a jacket
meant to be turned inside out.

  • SATIN SHELL

    Acetate satin, 180 gsm. Holds the sheen, not
    the creases – pressed once, worn for years.

  • YOKOSUKA STITCH

    Machine-guided by hand, the way Kiryu
    embroiderers did it in 1949. Thread, not print.
    Feel the relief.

  • FULLY REVERSIBLE

    Two shells, two motifs, one seam between
    them. Flip it in a doorway. Nobody sees the
    same jacket twice.

  • QUILTED BODY

    Diamond quilting through both layers keeps the
    satin flat and the shape sharp. No sag at the
    hem.
